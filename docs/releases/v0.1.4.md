Paper of any size, with the margins set in Word.

### Downloads
- **Windows:** `daisywriter-0.1.4-windows-x64.msi`. It replaces an earlier
  version if one is installed. Your browser and Windows may warn about it,
  because the installer isn't signed yet: see
  [installing](https://daisywriter.jfet.net/#download).
- **Mac** (Apple silicon): `daisywriter-0.1.4-macos-arm64.dmg`. Not signed yet,
  so the first time use **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Sample letters:** `daisywriter-0.1.4-samples.zip`.

The [user guide](https://daisywriter.jfet.net/#guide) is also in the app under
**Help > daisywriter User Guide** (F1).

### Changes
- **Paper size and margins come from Word.** Set them in the template with
  **Layout > Size** (**More Paper Sizes…** for sizes like 6⅛ × 11 inches) and
  **Layout > Margins**. The new **Paper** line at the top of the window shows
  what daisywriter found, and the preview shows the sheet at its size.
- The typewriter sets some limits: lines end no further than 7½ inches from
  the paper's left edge, and the top margin is at least ⅔ inch. For a left
  margin under 1 inch, daisywriter asks you to put each sheet a little to the
  right of the paper guide (or of 10 on the scale), and says how far. If a margin can't be used as
  set, daisywriter says so and uses the nearest one it can.
- Letters now follow the template's margins exactly. With Word's usual 1-inch
  margins, lines are one character shorter than before (65 instead of 66). A
  template with wider margins gets shorter lines than before.
- Command line: `--paper-size` (letter, legal, a4, or a size such as
  `6.125x11`) replaces `--stock`, and `--top`/`--bottom` default to the
  template's margins. `testpage left` tries typing left of the left margin.

### Known limits
- Only the Swintec 1146 CMP and Olympia Electronic Compact 2 so far.
- Paper wider than letter can't use the extra width yet.
- The installers aren't signed.
