More typewriters and special characters, and where to put the paper.

### Downloads
- **Windows:** `daisywriter-0.1.5-windows-x64.msi`. It replaces an earlier
  version if one is installed. Your browser and Windows may warn about it,
  because the installer isn't signed yet: see
  [installing](https://daisywriter.jfet.net/#download).
- **Mac** (Apple silicon): `daisywriter-0.1.5-macos-arm64.dmg`. Not signed yet,
  so the first time use **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Sample letters:** `daisywriter-0.1.5-samples.zip`.

The [user guide](https://daisywriter.jfet.net/#guide) is also in the app under
**Help > daisywriter User Guide** (F1).

### Changes
- **Special characters on the Swintec 1146 CMP:** • ² ³ ¢ ¼ ½ £ ¶ § ® ± °
  can now be typed. Before, a letter using them was refused.
- **Swintec 1146 CM** in the Typewriter list. Its firmware types some special
  characters differently from the 1146 CMP (on the CMP's settings, a | came out
  as ¶).
- **Olympia with a Swintec printwheel** in the Typewriter list. Swintec's
  replacement wheels have • where Olympia's has ©, and can type < and >.
- The Olympia's special characters, line length (65 characters) and bold have
  been checked on paper. Bold is now struck from the right, as on the Swintec,
  which makes it clearly darker on a carbon film ribbon.
- The user guide says which typewriter to choose, and where to set the paper
  guide: at 0 on an Olympia and 10 on a Swintec, for a 1-inch left margin. The
  paper prompt gives the mark for the chosen typewriter (before, it said 10 on
  the scale on an Olympia too).
- Pulling the USB cable while printing now always says the cable may be
  unplugged, instead of "Access is denied".

### Known limits
- Only the Swintec 1146 CMP and 1146 CM, and the Olympia Electronic Compact 2,
  so far.
- Paper wider than letter can't use the extra width yet.
- The installers aren't signed.
