Left margins under 1 inch, with the sheet against the paper guide.

### Downloads
- **Windows:** `daisywriter-0.1.7-windows-x64.msi`. It replaces an earlier
  version if one is installed. Your browser and Windows may warn about it,
  because the installer isn't signed yet: see
  [installing](https://daisywriter.jfet.net/#download).
- **Mac** (Apple silicon): `daisywriter-0.1.7-macos-arm64.dmg`. Not signed yet,
  so the first time use **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Sample letters:** `daisywriter-0.1.7-samples.zip`.

The [user guide](https://daisywriter.jfet.net/#guide) is also in the app under
**Help > daisywriter User Guide** (F1).

### Changes
- **Left margins under 1 inch** no longer need the sheet moved to the right of
  the paper guide. Each line starts with backspaces, which take the typewriter
  past its own left margin, so every sheet goes against the guide as usual.
  (Checked on the Olympia; the Swintec's firmware does the same.)
- Such a margin is typed to the nearest tenth of an inch: ¾ inch comes out at
  0.8 inch.

### Known limits
- Only the Swintec 1146 CMP and 1146 CM, and the Olympia Electronic Compact 2,
  so far.
- Paper wider than letter can't use the extra width yet.
- The installers aren't signed.
