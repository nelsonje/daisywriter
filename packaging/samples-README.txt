daisywriter sample letters

Files to try daisywriter with, before making your own. Unzip them into a folder,
start daisywriter, and choose:

  Template:  letter-template.docx
  Data:      letter-data.xlsx

letter-data.xlsx
    Five customers of a made-up typewriter repair shop: one letter each. The
    second customer has no company and no second address line, so those lines
    are left out of that letter. The third has accented letters, which the
    daisywheel may not have: daisywriter warns and types the nearest letter.

letter-template.docx
    The letter, with the customer's details filled in from the spreadsheet,
    bold, underline, a justified paragraph, an indented paragraph, a tab, and
    today's date.

letter-template-from-word.docx
    The same kind of letter typed in Microsoft Word with {{placeholders}},
    as you would make your own.

letter-template-markers.docx
    Like letter-template.docx, with {{body}} and {{/body}} around the part of
    the letter that may get typing errors (see "Typing errors" in the user
    guide).

To check a letter without paper, choose "Print on the emulator (PDF)" next to
the Print button. The user guide is in daisywriter under Help > User Guide (F1).
